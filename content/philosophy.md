## CN
04 / Founding Belief
创 始 之 言

“人生值得追求的事情，
都需要 情怀 · 品格 ·
感受 · 欢乐。”

—— 远方 · 创始人语

当教育变成纯粹的投资，只用业绩与规模衡量时，“教书育人”那份静静的美好便会遮蔽。我们不愿如此。我们坚持内心的静、喜悦与安顶。

人生多难处，最后让人穿越难关的，仅是真才、真品、真修养。人生多梦想，回首才发现 —— 所谓追求本身，就是梦想。

培育“渊博、坦荡、快乐”的青少年 —— 这是我们的事。我们不会改变世界。但当更多孩子因为我们而领略文学之美、品格之体、语言之能——他们反过来所创造的世界，也会因此更好一些。这就是花开远方。

## EN
04 / Founding Belief
Founding Belief

Every endeavour worth doing needs sentiment, integrity, feeling and joy — and so does a human life.

When education becomes pure investment, measured only in performance and scale, the quiet good of teaching and growing a young person is lost. We have chosen not to work that way. We hold to stillness, gladness and a settled mind.

Life has many hard crossings. In the end, only real talent, real character and real integrity see a person through. Life has many dreams worth chasing. Look back — the chasing itself was the dream.

To cultivate young people who are learned, open-minded and joyful — that is our work. We will not change the world. But when more young people, through our teaching, come to know the beauty of literature, the quiet bearing of a cultivated character, and the command of their own language — the world they make, in their turn, will be a better one. That is Blooming Future.
